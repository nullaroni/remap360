      Editing your Advantage2 in the browser

      1. Turn on Power User Mode: hold progm + Shift and tap Esc. The LEDs flash four times.
      2. Open the v-Drive: hold progm and tap F1. A drive called ADVANTAGE2 appears after a few seconds.
      3. Click Open v-Drive… and choose the drive (or its active folder). Allow editing when the browser asks.
      4. Change keys, then click Save.
      5. Eject the drive in Finder or Explorer, then press progm + F1 to close it. Changes take effect when the v-Drive closes. You can also reload the layout with progm + F3 (QWERTY) or F4 (Dvorak).

      Browsers: saving straight to the drive needs Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser. In Safari and Firefox, use Open File…, then Copy and paste the text into the file on the v-Drive with TextEdit or Notepad.

      Older keyboards with a 2 MB v-Drive: Kinesis recommends pasting new contents into the existing file instead of replacing it. Use Copy and paste into the open file to be safe.

      Before you start: your original file is backed up in this browser before every save, and you can download it from the Backups list below.
